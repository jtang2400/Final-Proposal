## Description
A looping animation about snowflakes with different shapes falling randomly and accumulating on the ground in successive layers. Once the screen is completely covered by snow, an image of the sun appears and melts it all away.

## Features
- Snow falling
	- Using digital rain to create snow falling.
- Snow accumulation effect
	- The alpha rate of snow accumulation will change from 0-1 from bottom to top.
- Melt snow 
	- After the snow covered all screen, play an animation of sun melt snow, then restart again.

## Challenges
- Different shapes of snow.
- Create loop of start sun melt animation and exit to begining.
- Snow accumulation appearence by time.

## Outcomes
Ideal Outcome:
- Have about 3 shapes of snow falling with curve path, more realistic snow accumulation, fluently play sun melt snow animation, successful back to looping.

Minimal Viable Outcome:
- Have 2 shapes of snow falling, have snow accumulation effect, can play sun melt snow animation, back to looping.
